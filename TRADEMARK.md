# Tesseract Trademark and Brand Guidelines

“Tesseract” is a trademark of Chrispian Burks / Hollis Labs.

This project is open source under the MIT License. You are free to use, modify,
and distribute the code for any purpose, including commercial use.

However, the **Tesseract name, logo, and branding are not covered by the MIT License**.

---

## Allowed Uses (No Permission Required)

You are encouraged to use Tesseract as a foundation for your own work.

The following uses are explicitly allowed:

- “Built with Tesseract”
- “Powered by Tesseract”
- “Runs on Tesseract”
- Attribution in documentation, UI, or marketing
- Internal use within your organization

These uses help grow the ecosystem and are encouraged but not required.

---

## Allowed Uses (With Conditions)

You may:

- Fork and modify the codebase
- Build commercial products using Tesseract
- White-label Tesseract as part of your own system
- Offer hosted or SaaS versions based on Tesseract

Conditions:

- You must not present your product as the official Tesseract distribution
- You must not imply endorsement or affiliation with the original project
- You must not use Tesseract branding in a way that causes confusion

Examples:

- ✅ “Acme AI Platform (Built with Tesseract)”
- ✅ “Custom AI System powered by Tesseract”
- ❌ “Tesseract Enterprise by Acme”
- ❌ “Official Tesseract Cloud”

---

## Prohibited Uses

The following are not allowed:

- Releasing a product named “Tesseract” that is not the official distribution
- Using the Tesseract name or branding as your product’s primary identity
- Misrepresenting a fork as the official Tesseract project
- Using logos or branding that are confusingly similar to Tesseract

---

## Official Distribution

Only builds released by Chrispian Burks / Hollis Labs (or explicitly designated
maintainers) may be referred to as:

- “Tesseract”
- “Official Tesseract”
- “Tesseract Platform”

---

## Naming Your Fork

If you create a fork or derivative, you must choose a different name.

You may reference Tesseract in a descriptive way, such as:

- “<Your Product>, built on Tesseract”
- “<Your Product>, powered by Tesseract”

---

## Logos and Visual Identity

If logos or brand assets are provided:

- They may only be used to refer to the official Tesseract project
- They may not be modified or reused as part of another product’s branding

---

## Why This Exists

Tesseract is designed to be:

- Open and extensible
- Commercially usable
- Easy to adopt and adapt

Trademark protection ensures:

- Users can identify the official distribution
- The ecosystem can grow without confusion
- The brand maintains a consistent meaning
